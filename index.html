about me

I studied psychology, then taught myself design.

Who I am

  • Art director
  • Creative lead
  • Founder, HER. Design Lab
  • Illustrator & author

I’m an art director and creative lead from Nairobi. I studied psychology at USIU-Africa, taught myself design, and have worked in agencies since 2019 on banks, beer, beauty and public health. I was deputy creative director at DataMuse, leading a team of 10, and now run my own studio, HER. Design Lab.

Growing up in Nairobi you switch languages a few times a day, and the slang changes before you’ve learned it. So every brief starts the same way for me: find out how the people on the other end live and talk. That’s how I approached a savings product for small-business owners in Uganda, and AVAC’s HIV-prevention campaign, which ran in 10 countries across Africa, Asia and South America.

In 2025 my team, Cool Waters, won Cannes Young Lions Kenya, and I became the first Kenyan selected for the Cannes Lions Creative Academy 30 Under 30. In 2026 I sat on the Young Lions Kenya design jury.
